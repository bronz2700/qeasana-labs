Zombie Detour Privacy Policy

A Qeasana Labs game

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Zombie Detour keeps your progress on your phone. The only things that ever leave it are the name you choose, your score, the stage you reached and a random player number, sent to our online leaderboard so the high-score table works. No ads, no tracking, no email, no location, nothing sold.

Who we are

Zombie Detour is a game made by Qeasana Labs. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. If you have any questions, or want your data deleted, email us at samvitin27@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress on your phone: your best score and best stage, your coins, the runners and items you own and wear, your sound and vibration settings, the name you choose, and a random player number the game makes up the first time it runs. This save never leaves your phone except for the leaderboard details below. Uninstalling the game (or clearing its data) deletes it.

The online leaderboard

When a run ends, the game sends your score to our online high-score table so you can see how you rank. It sends: your random player number, the name you chose, your score and the stage you reached. When you open the leaderboard, it sends your random player number to fetch the table and mark your own row.

Your name and score are shown to everyone who opens the leaderboard, so please don't use your full real name. You can change your name at any time under Settings > Change name.

The random player number is made up by the game. It isn't your phone's ID, your Google account or anything else that could identify you on its own.

Who handles the leaderboard

The leaderboard is run for us by Talo (trytalo.com), a game backend service, which stores it on servers in Germany. Like any website or online service, Talo's servers receive your internet (IP) address when the game connects, and Talo says it uses it to work out an approximate country and region. We only use the leaderboard to show the high-score table. Talo's own privacy policy is at https://trytalo.com/privacy.

All leaderboard traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).

What we don't collect

The game doesn't ask for or collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, advertising ID or your phone's hardware IDs. It has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases: coins are only earned by playing. We never sell or rent any information, and we don't share it with anyone except Talo as described above, or if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

Leaderboard entries are kept for as long as the leaderboard runs, or until you ask us to delete them. Everything else stays on your phone until you delete it.

Deleting your data

To have your leaderboard entry deleted, email samvitin27@gmail.com with the name you played under (and roughly your score and when you played, so we can find it). We'll delete it within 30 days. To delete everything on your phone, uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings.

Children

Zombie Detour isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has put their name on the leaderboard, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it collects, we'll update this policy and the date at the top before the change goes out.
