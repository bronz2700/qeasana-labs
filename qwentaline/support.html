How do I back up my data or move it to a new phone?

iPhone: with iCloud Drive turned on, Qwentaline backs up to iCloud Drive → Qwentaline every day by itself. On the new iPhone (signed in with the same Apple Account), install Qwentaline, tap Restore and pick the latest backup.

Android, or any phone: tap Backup at the top and save the file (Files, Google Drive, email to yourself, AirDrop). On the new phone, install Qwentaline and tap Restore, then choose that file. Android's own Google backup (Settings → Google → Backup) also includes Qwentaline's data when it's on.

How do I import a credit card statement?

Save the PDF statement from your bank's email to your phone, then tap Import statement and choose it. If the PDF has a password, enter it once — Qwentaline can remember it for that bank. The password stays on your phone and is never included in backups.

A statement didn't read correctly. What can I do?

Check the details on the review screen and correct anything that's off before saving. Banks change their statement layouts; email us the bank name and card type (not the PDF) and we'll look into it.

How do recurring expenses work?

On Income or Expenses, tap + Recurring for rent, salaries (monthly, twice a month, weekly) or subscriptions. Each one is added automatically on its due date; edit a single entry if the amount differs that month.

How do I delete all my data?

Scroll to the bottom and tap Erase all data, or delete the app. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them.
