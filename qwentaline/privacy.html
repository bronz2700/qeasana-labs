Qwentaline privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Qwentaline, made by Qeasana Labs, helps you track what customers owe you, your income and expenses, your credit cards and your loans. It is built so that your information stays on your own device.

What Qwentaline stores

Everything you enter or import — invoices, payments, income and expense entries, card and loan details, imported statement details, receipt photos and screenshots, and card pictures — is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. If you save a password for opening statement PDFs, it is also stored only on your device and is never included in backup files.

What Qwentaline collects

Nothing. Qwentaline has no account or sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see your data. Statement PDFs are read on your device; they are not uploaded anywhere.

Sharing

Your data leaves your device only when you choose to: for example when you tap Backup or Export CSV and send or save the file somewhere with your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes is up to you.

Optional account and sync

If you create an account to sync your devices, Qwentaline stores your email address and your records on the Qwentaline sync server. Your records are encrypted on your device with a key made from your password before they are uploaded, and the password itself is never sent, so the server — and Qeasana Labs — cannot read them. The server keeps only the encrypted records, your email, and a one-way check of a value derived from your password. Your email address is used only to send you verification and password-reset codes, which are delivered by our email provider, Resend; it is never used for marketing or shared with anyone else. Accounts whose email was never confirmed are removed after 7 days. You can delete your account and all its online records at any time in Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. If you don't create an account, nothing leaves your device.

Connecting your own point-of-sale system

If you choose to connect Qwentaline to your own POS (for example Keasana POS), it uses an access key you create in that POS to read your closed days' sales totals, expenses, utang collected and who owes you (customer names and amounts) directly from your POS server. The key is stored only on your device and never included in backup files. Nothing is sent to the developer, and you can disconnect or revoke the key at any time.

Backups to your own iCloud or Google account

On iPhone, if iCloud Drive is turned on, Qwentaline saves a daily backup file to your own iCloud Drive (in a folder named Qwentaline). You can turn this off under Backup in the app. On Android, your phone's own backup service may include Qwentaline's data in your Google account backup, depending on your phone's settings. These backups are stored in your Apple or Google account under Apple's or Google's terms; the developer has no access to them.

Photos and camera

If you attach a receipt, a payment screenshot or a picture of your card, Qwentaline asks for access to your photos or camera only for that purpose. The picture is kept on your device and in backup files you make.

Deleting your data

Use “Erase all data” in the app, or delete the app. Backup files you made, including the daily backups in iCloud Drive → Qwentaline, are kept until you delete them (in the Files app for iCloud Drive).

Children

Qwentaline is meant for adults managing their own finances and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
